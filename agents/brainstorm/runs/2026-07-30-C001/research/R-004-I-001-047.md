## Idea echoed
- **Title:** Design/review duality wave shape — audit → propose → gate, with acceptance criteria pre-registered before the fix.
- **Description:** A UX design/review loop must both propose designs (divergent) and judge them (critical). Wave shape = **audit** (defects w/ evidence) → **propose** (author a change) → **gate** (verify vs criteria). Load-bearing addition: acceptance criteria are **pre-registered and frozen before the fix is authored**, so the proposer can't relax the bar it's later graded against. Also merged: an adversary persona at a different temperature rewarded for finding one concrete defect, where "looks fine" is a null result, not a pass.
- **Riskiest assumption:** A single loop can design *and* review without self-grading drift — that pre-registration + persona separation is sufficient structural defence when proposer and judge are the same model in the same run.
- **Warrant:** cd-review already separates audit from fix and gates on external verification (exit codes); pre-registration adds a guard code fixes get for free (objective tests) that design fixes lack.

## Steelman (1 paragraph)
The duality shape is well-motivated: refusing to reuse brainstorm's α→β→γ or cd-review's audit→fix unchanged is correct, because a design loop's two jobs genuinely conflict. Pre-registration is a *real* debiasing move imported from a domain built to defeat exactly this failure (outcome-switching in trials). Freezing criteria before authoring removes the single most exploitable degree of freedom — moving the goalposts — and the adversary-persona-with-null-result reframing attacks confirmation bias from the other side by making "I found nothing" the failure mode for the critic, not the proposer. In a single-model shop with no browser/visual tooling and no HITL in the worker, structural anti-sycophancy is the only lever available, and this stacks two independent structural guards rather than relying on the model's goodwill.

## Toulmin decomposition
- **Claim:** Pre-registered/frozen criteria + a differently-tempered adversary persona are sufficient structural defence to make a same-model, same-run propose→gate loop produce an honest gate.
- **Grounds:** cd-review separates audit (Wave D lenses) from fix (Wave C) [`agents/cd-review/LOOP.md:496`, `:526`]; its gate's *objective* half (`npm run check` = lint + `tsc --noEmit`, `npm run test`) runs at §7.3 **before** any subjective review [`LOOP.md:489-494`]; Wave D is explicitly *not* the verify step [`LOOP.md:551`, `REVIEW-LENS.md:362-363`] and its reviewers are **separate subagents** that "do not see each other" [`LOOP.md:521-524`, `REVIEW-LENS.md:9-10`].
- **Warrant:** Freezing the bar before authoring closes the goalpost-moving degree of freedom, so a self-graded gate becomes honest.
- **Backing:** Pre-registration reduces outcome-flexibility bias in its home domain (trials/registered reports).
- **Qualifier:** Holds only if self-preference bias is *caused by* criterion flexibility rather than by the evaluator recognising its own output.
- **Rebuttal:** Self-preference bias persists even when criteria are *entirely objective and programmatically verifiable* [2], and is causally driven by self-recognition [3] — a channel pre-registration does not touch.

## Falsification attempt
- **Falsifier proposed:** If self-preference bias survives when the acceptance criteria are fixed/objective — i.e. an evaluator grading against a bar it cannot move still systematically passes its own output — then frozen pre-registration is not sufficient structural defence and the duality gate is not honest as specified.
- **Falsifier tested via:** (a) ExaSearch on LLM self-preference / LLM-as-judge bias → arxiv 2604.06996 [2], 2404.13076 [3], 2410.21819 [4]; (b) ExaSearch on self-correction limits → arxiv 2310.01798 [5]; (c) repo grep of cd-review gate structure [1].
- **Outcome: CONFIRMED falsifier.** [2] tests the strongest version of the guard the idea proposes (criteria fixed *and objective*, stronger than merely pre-registered) and finds SPB persists: "among rubrics where generators fail, judges can be **up to 50% more likely to incorrectly mark them as satisfied when the output is their own.**" [3] shows the mechanism is self-*recognition* (causal, resists confounders) — orthogonal to criterion flexibility, so pre-registration cannot reach it. cd-review's own gate concedes this by keeping the objective half (exit codes) as a *separate, prior* stage authored by a *different* subagent [1] — the exact externalisation this same-run idea drops.

## Position-swap reconciliation
- **Supporter reading:** Pre-registration + adversary persona is strictly better than nothing; [2] also shows ensembling judges mitigates SPB, so the shape is on the right track and just needs tuning.
- **Detractor reading:** The crux assumption is directly refuted — bias survives objective criteria [2] and is recognition-driven [3]; and Exigo is single-model, so it cannot even use the one partial mitigation [2] offers (judge ensembling across families). A UX gate lacks cd-review's objective exit-code anchor entirely [1], so it inherits *only* the subjective half that [2] shows leaks.
- **Reconciliation:** Detractor is better supported by the fetched evidence. The idea's *directional* value (audit→propose→gate shape, adversary/null-result reframing) survives; its *load-bearing sufficiency claim* — that freezing criteria makes a same-run self-graded gate honest — is contradicted by primary evidence that targets precisely that guard.

## Verdict
- **Verdict:** REFUTE
- **Confidence:** 0.72
- **Justification:** The riskiest assumption is that pre-registration + persona separation suffices when proposer = judge = same model, same run; direct empirical evidence [2] shows self-preference bias survives even *objective* criteria (a stronger guard than pre-registration) and is caused by self-recognition [3], a channel pre-registration cannot touch. Confidence is 0.72 not higher because the papers measure LLM-as-judge scoring, not this exact author-then-gate wave, and the adversary-persona reframing is a partially independent guard the SPB studies did not test.

## Constraint for next cycle
- **Constraint text:** The gate MUST be externalised — the acceptance check must run in a separate turn/persona-with-no-authorship-memory or against a signal the proposer cannot author (deterministic assertion, fixture, or diff-of-behaviour) — because pre-registering criteria alone does not stop same-run self-preference bias.
