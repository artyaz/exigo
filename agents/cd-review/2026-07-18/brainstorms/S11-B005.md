## Decision Package — S11-B005

### Recommendation
- **Approach name:** B — Keep playground dark-chrome CSS as intentional, but unify inject helper; settings should match product Tailwind pages
- **Rationale:** Playground intentionally mirrors a design-system mono chrome (commented in source). Converting hundreds of BEM rules to Tailwind in one pass is high churn/low clarity win. Settings is a product surface (AI keys) and already small — Tailwind aligns with pricing/checkout. A tiny `injectScopedStyles(id, css)` fixes race/leak differences.
- **Why not alternatives:** Full Tailwind conversion of playground (A) is a large rewrite. Leaving as-is (C) keeps four slightly different inject implementations.
