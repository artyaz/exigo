## Recommendation
- Approach name: **A — Server-derived identity only (match exerciseComments)**
- One-paragraph rationale: The advanced in-repo pattern is `getAuthedContext` / `getAuthenticatedUserId` with no client userId. Dropping args shortens handlers, removes a class of identity-mismatch bugs, and makes reviews trivial (“is auth called?”). Apply across `spaces.ts` and `tests.ts` public functions; update React `useQuery`/`useMutation` call sites to omit userId.
- Why not B (keep dual check forever): perpetuates the smell that caused questions/userSettings P0s.
