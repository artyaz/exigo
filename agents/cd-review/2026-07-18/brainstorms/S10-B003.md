## Decision Package — S10-B003

### Recommendation
- Approach name: **Surgical dead-scaffold strip of tRPC from the app shell, keep packages until S9/orchestrator deletes Prisma stack**
- One-paragraph rationale: `TRPCReactProvider` wraps every page via `src/app/layout.tsx`, yet the only consumer is T3 demo `src/app/_components/post.tsx` (and Prisma `post` router). Convex is the primary API. Keeping a full tRPC + React Query + SuperJSON provider tree on the critical path is dead weight for readability and bundle clarity. Removing the provider from the shell (and deleting unused `src/trpc/*` exports from layout) is the S10-owned win; full package/deps removal can follow in a later pack that owns `src/server/api/**`.
- Why not the alternatives: Leaving tRPC “for later” keeps the liar architecture. Big-bang delete of Prisma+tRPC+deps in one PR is correct eventually but spans many slices and risks CI breakage.
