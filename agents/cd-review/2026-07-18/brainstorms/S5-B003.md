## Decision Package — S5-B003

### Recommendation
- **Approach name:** Small `display/envEval.ts` with 4 primitives
- **One-paragraph rationale:** Every display layer reimplements “run expr against env, swallow errors, coerce type.” That is pure logic (no React), easy to unit-test, and the current scatter makes the paint-time defense contract hard to see. Extract `safeRun`, `valNum`, `valStr`, `valList` (and keep plot-specific `asPoints` next to Plot or as `valPoints`). Do not force all layers into one Component factory — only the eval glue.
- **Why not the alternatives:** A big Display-layer plugin framework is speculative. Leaving duplicates is the opposite of brevity.
