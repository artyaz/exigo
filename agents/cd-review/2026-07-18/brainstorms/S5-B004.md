## Decision Package — S5-B004

### Recommendation
- **Approach name:** Align names with embed; share `extractFencedHtml`
- **One-paragraph rationale:** Two unrelated types share `OpenExerciseResult`. Importing from constructor vs OpenExercise is a footgun; the barrel exports the client type, so server callers use a different shape under the same name. Mirror embed’s naming: client completion → `OpenResult` (or keep `OpenExerciseResult` only for completion and rename authoring to `OpenAuthoringResult`). Extract shared `extractFencedHtml(raw)` to a tiny pure helper under open or a shared exercises util — the functions are line-for-line identical.
- **Why not the alternatives:** Leaving the collision is a permanent clarity tax. Merging open/embed authoring APIs is product scope.
