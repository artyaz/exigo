Extract pure/unit-testable pieces without changing runtime behavior: (1) `tutorTools.ts` declarations + executeTool, (2) `tutorContext.ts` assembleContext + embedding helper, (3) `tutorMemory.ts` extract-and-store, (4) keep stream orchestration in route or `tutorStream.ts`.
