## Idea echoed
"Blind externalised gate": split capture/gate; gate sees only artifact bytes + mechanical claim (no proposer prose); scores surface-under-review vs. known-good clean twin **blind to which is which**; indistinguishable twins = bench failure. Risk: narration-withholding strips self-recognition.

## Steelman
If recognition rides on authorship cues in the prose ("I made this cleaner"), deleting prose and feeding only pixels+a11y-tree removes the label, collapsing the judge to content-only. The blind A/B against a known-good twin converts bias-prone absolute scoring into forced discrimination with a ground-truth anchor: a self-preferring judge must still pick the actually-cleaner surface or be caught failing the twin.

## Toulmin decomposition
- **Claim:** Blinding to authorship strips the self-recognition channel, externalising the gate.
- **Grounds:** Self-recognition causally drives self-preference [C1]. BUT the mechanism is **perplexity/familiarity**: judges favour low-perplexity text "regardless of whether outputs were self-generated" [C4]. Bias is worst when "objective criteria" are absent [C2]. Pairwise-vs-reference beats absolute pointwise for *visual* outputs; off-the-shelf models then outperform top-tier [C5].
- **Warrant:** If recognition needs the authorship *label*, removing prose removes it.
- **Backing:** C1 fine-tunes a linear self-recognition→bias link "resisting confounders."
- **Qualifier:** Holds only if recognition is presentation-borne, not content-borne.
- **Rebuttal:** C4 shows the signal is intrinsic to the *bytes* (perplexity/familiarity of a11y-tree text + image), present even without prose. C6 warns measurements carry ~89.6%-reducible confound.

## Falsification attempt
Prediction: give one judge (i) artifact+prose, (ii) artifact-only. If self-preference (marking own failing output "satisfied") barely drops in (ii), blinding didn't strip the channel → falsifier **fires**. C4's perplexity result predicts exactly this: the familiarity signal lives in the bytes, so narration-blinding is largely inert against leg (b). Leg (c) survives on separate grounds [C5], but the stated rationale is unsupported.

## Position-swap reconciliation
**Supporter:** leg (c) is the real externaliser — blind pairwise-vs-known-good beats absolute scoring for visual artifacts [C5]; the twin is an exit-code-like anchor.
**Detractor:** leg (b) refuted by C4 — content not authorship carries recognition; C6 says self-preference measurement is confound-laden; pairwise still flips ~13.6% [C7].
**Reconcile:** the gate's *justification* is wrong (blinding≠stripping recognition) but its *strongest leg* (blind pairwise-vs-oracle-twin) has independent support. Mixed, not clean.

## Verdict
**INCONCLUSIVE** — confidence 0.72. The stated causal claim (leg b) is refuted by primary sources; the surviving leg (c) is real but unproven for *this* self-preference setting. Not an ADVANCE (rationale falsified); not a REFUTE (twin-anchor leg is evidence-backed).
