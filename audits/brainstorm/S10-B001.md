## Decision Package — S10-B001

### Recommendation
- Approach name: **Expand protected matchers to all session-owned app surfaces**
- One-paragraph rationale: Middleware currently only gates `/spaces(.*)` and `/tests(.*)`. Auth-sensitive surfaces that live under other paths (`/settings` for AI keys, `/knowledge-nodes`, `/checkout`, likely `/playground`) rely on ad-hoc server-action checks or client `SignedIn`/`useAuth` alone. Edge protection is the Exigo convention stated in AGENTS.md; expanding the matcher is the smallest change that restores a single, readable auth boundary without inventing a new framework.
- Why not the alternatives: Full “protect everything except public allowlist” is cleaner long-term but larger blast radius for public marketing/pricing/legal routes. Leaving protection only in server actions is the status quo and keeps the middleware a liar about what is “protected.”

### Minimal implementation sketch
- Files to touch: `src/middleware.ts` (S10); optionally add comments listing public routes.
- Steps:
  1. Expand `isProtectedRoute` matchers to include session-owned pages: at minimum `/settings(.*)`, `/knowledge-nodes(.*)`, `/checkout(.*)`, `/playground(.*)`.
  2. Confirm public routes remain open: `/`, `/pricing`, `/terms`, `/sign-in`, `/sign-up`, `/sso-callback`, marketing assets.
  3. Manually hit `/settings` signed-out → expect Clerk protect redirect (same as `/spaces`).
- What NOT to do: Do not rewrite middleware into a custom RBAC layer; do not gate public API webhooks.
