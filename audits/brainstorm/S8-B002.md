## Decision Package — S8-B002

### Recommendation
- **Approach name:** A — Thin shared Gemini helpers for the raw-GoogleGenAI routes; leave `resolveAiProvider` as the exercise-playground stack
- **One-paragraph rationale:** There are two intentional product paths: (1) product AI (learn/tests/knowledge) using Convex prompts + GoogleGenAI + PostHog, (2) playground/exercise authoring via `resolveAiProvider` (user BYO keys). Collapsing (1) into (2) mixes plan/provider concerns with streaming product routes. Extract `src/lib/geminiServer.ts` (or `src/server/ai/geminiRoute.ts`) with `getGeminiClient()`, `getDefaultModel()`, `withGeminiRetries()`, optional `captureStreamGeneration(...)` wrapper. Port generate’s retry into teach/clarify/tutor/feels-hard/validate primary path. Align PostHog fields (stream flag, TTFT when streaming).
- **Why not alternatives:** B (full migrate to resolveAiProvider) is a large design fork and loses clear PostHog path. C (do nothing) leaves AGENTS.md lying.
